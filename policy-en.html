1.5. The Policy does not apply to third-party services linked from the Service (for example, Google, Telegram or the payment service). They process data under their own rules.

2. Definitions

  • Personal data: any information relating directly or indirectly to an identified or identifiable individual (the data subject).
  • Processing: any action or set of actions performed on personal data, including collection, recording, organisation, accumulation, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, transfer, blocking, erasure and destruction.
  • User: an individual who uses the Service.
  • Account: the user’s record in the Service, created when signing up with an email address or a Google account.
  • Shared room: a common task list that can be joined by users who know the room code.

3. Data we process

3.1. Data you provide

  • Sign-up and sign-in: email address and password. The password is stored by the authentication service only as a hash and is not available to the Operator. When signing in with Google: name, email address and profile photo from the Google account.
  • Profile: the name and photo you set or change in the App.
  • Content: tasks, descriptions, categories, due dates, reminders, attached photos and audio recordings.
  • Study data: the university and study group you select, and a photo or file of your syllabus uploaded for recognition.
  • Shared rooms: tasks, comments, reactions, attached files and task assignments.
  • Telegram link: your Telegram user ID and time zone, and the messages and files you send to the Bot.
  • Payments: the email address for the receipt and the chosen plan. Bank card details are entered on the payment service’s page; the Operator neither receives nor stores them.
  • Website request form: university name, city, study group and a contact for the reply (email address or Telegram username).
  • Inquiries: the content of emails you send to the Operator.

3.2. Data collected automatically

  • account identifier;
  • device identifier for push notifications;
  • “online” status and last activity time in a shared room;
  • subscription details: plan, expiry date, payment ID;
  • the number of requests to artificial intelligence features per billing period and, for requests made without signing in (for example, from the demo assistant on the Website), the IP address;
  • server logs: IP address, date and time, request type, error details.

3.3. What we do not collect

The Service does not collect location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, data from other apps or the advertising ID, and does not use web analytics or advertising trackers. Special categories of personal data (Article 10 of Law No. 152-FZ) and biometric personal data (Article 11 of Law No. 152-FZ) are not processed.

3.4. Microphone and camera

The microphone and camera are used only when you act: recording audio, dictating a task or taking a photo. Voice input is handled by the device’s system speech recognition service under its developer’s rules; the App receives only the recognised text.

8. Shared rooms

8.1. All members of a shared room can see the room’s tasks, comments, reactions and files, as well as other members’ names, profile photos and online status. Anyone who knows the room code can join; the code is shared by users themselves.

8.2. Room administrators can remove members and files from the room. A user can leave a room at any time.

8.3. Attached files open via a direct link. The link is not published, but anyone it is shared with can open the file.

9. Cookies and on-device storage

9.1. The Website does not use cookies for analytics or advertising. Browser local storage keeps only your chosen colour theme; you can remove it by clearing the site data in your browser.

9.2. The App keeps a copy of your data on the device to work offline and caches downloaded photos. When you sign out, local data is removed from the device.

10. Retention and deletion

  • Account and content: for as long as the account exists. After a deletion request or withdrawal of consent, data is destroyed within 30 days (Part 5 of Article 21 of Law No. 152-FZ) unless the law requires otherwise.
  • Payment details: for the periods required by Russian tax legislation.
  • Requests and inquiries: until answered, then deleted.
  • Artificial intelligence request counts: for the plan’s billing period.
  • Server logs: no longer than needed for security, usually a few days.

Once the purposes of processing are achieved, personal data is destroyed or anonymised.

11. Your rights

11.1. Under Articles 14–17 and 21 of Law No. 152-FZ, you have the right to:

  • obtain information about the processing of your personal data: the fact and purposes of processing, the data processed, retention periods and recipients;
  • request correction, blocking or destruction of data that is incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or no longer needed for the stated purposes;
  • withdraw your consent to processing;
  • appeal against the Operator’s actions to Roskomnadzor, the Russian data protection authority (rkn.gov.ru), or in court.

11.2. You can change or delete some data yourself in the App: your profile, tasks and attachments, shared room membership and the Telegram link.

11.3. Send requests and consent withdrawals to chuprinapolina212@gmail.com from the email address of your account, or state that address, so the Operator can identify you. The Operator replies within 10 business days of receiving the request. This period may be extended by no more than 5 business days, with notice of the reasons.

11.4. After consent is withdrawn, the Operator stops processing and destroys the data within 30 days unless there is another legal ground for processing. The Service cannot be used without processing some data (for example, the email address and content).

12. Data security

12.1. The Operator takes legal, organisational and technical measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure and destruction (Articles 18.1 and 19 of Law No. 152-FZ):

  • data is transmitted only over encrypted channels (HTTPS, TLS);
  • cloud database access rules let users see only their own data and the data of rooms they belong to;
  • server functions accept requests only with a valid authorisation token;
  • passwords are stored only as hashes;
  • service access keys are kept in a secure secrets store and are not included in the App;
  • only the minimum necessary data is shared with third-party services.

12.2. If an unlawful transfer of personal data that violates users’ rights is detected, the Operator notifies Roskomnadzor within the periods set by Part 3.1 of Article 21 of Law No. 152-FZ.

13. Children

The Service is intended for persons over 14. Children under 14 may use the Service only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian. If the Operator learns that it has received data of a child under 14 without such consent, it will delete it.
